Samsung Smart Fridges Bricked After Update

24 sept 2026 2 min de lectura 👁

Samsung smart refrigerator owners in several countries recently faced an unexpected crisis when their high-end appliances suddenly stopped working after receiving firmware updates. The affected models, primarily Bespoke AI series four-door refrigerators produced in 2024 or later, experienced complete system failures immediately after attempting to update through Samsung’s SmartThings platform.

The technical glitch has left thousands of customers without refrigeration just as Samsung’s home country of Korea entered the mid-autumn holiday season. Reports indicate that the SmartThings application shows these disconnected devices as offline, with no apparent way to revive them through normal troubleshooting methods. Many users have been forced to discard all perishable food items stored in their malfunctioning appliances.

Samsung has acknowledged the issue in an official statement, confirming that the problem affects certain Bespoke AI models after recent firmware updates. The company has not yet identified the root cause of the failures but has indicated that the issue may be related to how the new firmware interacts with specific hardware configurations in these premium models.

Adding to consumer frustration, Samsung has informed affected customers that repair personnel may not be available until after the holiday period ends. This delay comes at an inopportune time when many families are preparing for extended gatherings and celebrations requiring reliable kitchen appliances.

The incident highlights potential vulnerabilities in increasingly connected home appliances, particularly those with automatic update systems. Smart home technology promises convenience but carries risks when software failures can render expensive equipment useless. This case underscores the importance of robust testing for firmware updates, especially for critical home appliances.

Industry experts note that while smart refrigerators represent a growing segment of the home appliance market, consumers remain concerned about reliability and long-term support for connected features. Samsung’s response to this widespread issue will likely impact consumer confidence in smart home technology across multiple brands and product categories.
